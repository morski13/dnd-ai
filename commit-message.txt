Battle engine phase 1: the rules core, and save spells marked at the table

- lib/engine: what conditions really do (advantage / disadvantage with reasons, auto-crit within 5 ft,
  auto-fail STR/DEX saves, speed, can't act), Exhaustion by rule set (2014 levels, 2024 −2 per level),
  damage types vs resistances / immunities / vulnerabilities ("nonmagical" too), condition immunities,
  timed effects (end / start of a turn, rounds, 1 minute, save ends, concentration).
- Sheet rolls follow the rules: attack, save and check advantage from your and the target's conditions,
  reasons shown under the roll; saves the app rolls for targets too.
- Damage lands with the target's defenses ("Ogre is resistant to fire").
- Turns tick timed conditions: they run out, repeat saves are rolled (monsters secretly);
  losing concentration ends what it kept up.
- Conditions with a duration on the Session screen and the sheet (Hold Person: Paralyzed, WIS save ends).
- Save spells at the table: "They failed / They saved"; a save means half damage (or none).
- SQL 35-timed-effects.sql.
