Battle map: light, darkvision, smoke, magical darkness and fog of war

- lib/engine/vision.ts: senses from stat blocks ("Darkvision 60 ft.; Blindsight 30 ft.") and from
  characters (features, else species: Drow 120, Elf / Dwarf / Tiefling… 60), light map (map light
  bright / dim / dark + torches: 20 ft bright, 20 ft dim, not through walls), what a creature sees
  (walls and smoke block, darkness needs darkvision, magical darkness needs truesight, blindsight
  sees close things, invisible needs blindsight / truesight).
- Terrain kinds: Smoke / fog, Magical darkness, Light (torch).
- Attacks (sheet and DM monsters): can't see the target = disadvantage, the target can't see you =
  advantage, with reasons.
- Fog of war (DM switch): players get only the squares their characters see and only the monsters
  in them (the server leaves the others out).
- Map shading: dim and dark squares, smoke; DM light tools (bright / dim / dark, fog of war).
