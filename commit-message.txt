Aim from the full sheet, on-the-spot fight builder with monster search and live odds

Character sheet
- Heals, buffs and (during a fight) attacks, damage and enemy spells ask who
  they go to: "Who gets Healing Word?", "Who does Morningstar hit?". Hits are
  checked against that creature's AC and damage goes on its HP. Area spells
  (Fireball) pick several; Mass Healing Word heals several.
- Heals on party characters apply outside sessions and fights too.
- Players only see characters players made or imported, plus whoever is in
  the running fight; monsters show how hurt they look, never their HP, and
  ones hidden on the battle map stay out. Checked on the server as well.

Session mode (DM)
- Build a fight on the spot: pick the players, search the monster library,
  add homebrew monsters and NPCs (as enemy or ally, with counts) and your own
  characters (on the party's side). Copies are made for library and homebrew
  monsters ("Goblin 1", "Goblin 2").
- DM-only odds preview of the planned fight (win / someone drops / death),
  redone after every change; the same builder adds to a running fight.
- "Roll for monsters & your characters" also rolls the DM's own characters.
- Turn card: spells that pick several creatures allow several targets.
