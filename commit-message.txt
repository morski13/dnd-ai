Battle map terrain: kinds, height, brush, doors and windows, hazards, falling

- lib/engine/terrain.ts: wall, tree, boulder, bush, tall grass, rocks, shallow / deep water, barrels,
  crates, difficult, fire, lava, spikes, acid; heights in feet; doors and windows; terrain v2 storage
  (the old walls / difficult lists still read); brush strokes; falling dice.
- space.ts: trees and boulders block moving and give up to three-quarters cover; bushes, barrels,
  crates and windows half cover; closed doors block sight; climbing costs a foot per foot; height
  counts in distance; ground higher than both creatures blocks the view; a solid wall along a grid
  line blocks too.
- DM brush (drag to paint, 1×1 or 3×3), paint height, doors and windows; doors open and close
  (players on their turn, next to it).
- Fire, lava, acid hurt when entering or starting a turn there, spikes for every square; a drop of
  10 ft or more is a fall (1d6 per 10 ft, Prone). Damage uses resistances and is logged.
- public/terrain/ waits for the DM's own pictures (TERRAIN_PICTURES switch); colours until then.
