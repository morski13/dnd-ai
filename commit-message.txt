Battle engine phase 3: the reaction pop-up (5 seconds, only for those who can react)

- lib/engine/react.ts: who can react to what. An attack hits: Shield (says when it makes it miss),
  Uncanny Dodge, Defensive Duelist, Parry, a monster's own reactions; friends: Silvery Barbs (60 ft),
  Interception (next to the target), Sentinel. A spell: Counterspell within 60 ft. Leaving reach:
  Opportunity Attack (War Caster). Damage: Hellish Rebuke, Absorb Elements.
- reaction_windows (SQL 38) opened by: the DM's monster attacks and damage on characters, players'
  attacks on monsters, players' spells (monsters with Counterspell), moves that leave reach.
  combatants.reaction_used: used for the round, back at the start of the creature's turn.
- Pop-up on the Session screen and the sheet: countdown, options with why, Pass. Shield adds +5 AC
  (later attacks too) and can turn the hit into a miss; Uncanny Dodge halves the next damage;
  Absorb Elements turns the damage to resisted; Opportunity Attack, Counterspell, Hellish Rebuke…
  are rolled from the sheet at whoever set it off. The DM answers for monsters.
- Monster card waits for the reactions before the next roll and shows what was used.
