Battle maps: terrain is set up in the map editor; objects of any size and height

- Battle maps → Terrain: Objects (tree, boulder, bush, rocks, barrels, crates, light) placed by
  tapping, 5 ft to 30 ft wide (1×1 to 6×6 squares) and as tall as you want; tap one to resize,
  change its height or remove it. Ground: the brush (walls, grass, water, hazards, smoke, darkness,
  heights). Light: bright / dim / dark and fog of war. Save terrain; fights on the map see it at once.
- Objects' rules cover every square they take (a 10-ft tree blocks 2×2), a light shines from its
  middle, flying over uses the object's own height.
- The session map no longer paints; the DM gets a link to the map editor.
