Battle map: flying (altitude, fly speeds, flying over things, falls)

- lib/engine/flight.ts: fly speed from stat blocks ("fly 60 ft. (hover)"), character features
  ("flying speed of 30 feet", "equal to your walking speed") or species (Aarakocra, Fairy, Owlin);
  heights of trees (20 ft) and boulders (10 ft); up / down cost; when a flyer falls.
- Moving in the air: flying speed, over trees and boulders when high enough, ground terrain doesn't
  slow, walls still block; height counts in distance and reach (melee can't reach a flyer 15 ft up).
- flyTo (up, down, land: 5 ft of movement per 5 ft), setFlySpeed (a flying speed for now: Fly spell),
  checkFalls after conditions change, at 0 HP and on turn changes: Prone / speed 0 / held / flight
  gone → falls (1d6 per 10 ft, Prone) unless it hovers. Ground hazards skip flyers.
- Map: altitude badge on tokens, flying controls in the token panel.
