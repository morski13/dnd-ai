Pick who a heal or buff goes to from the character sheet

Casting Healing Word, Cure Wounds, Mass Healing Word, Bless, Aid, Haste and the
like from the sheet now asks "Who gets ...?" with the party and their HP. Healing
changes the target's HP right away, in or out of a session or fight; buffs are
logged on the creatures picked. The tray shows "Orrin +15 HP (0 -> 15 / 31)".

- lib/targets.ts: aimOf (which options ask, one or several, extra creatures
  from a higher slot), mostHurt (default pick for heals); tests
- server: heals on party characters apply without a running session
- sheet: TargetPick dialog, party list from getCharacterSheet
