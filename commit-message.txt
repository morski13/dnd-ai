Combat mode for fights played at the table, fighting from the full sheet

- DM: "Start combat mode" in Session (no monsters needed). The party joins, players roll initiative
  from their sheets and end their own turns; the DM can add enemies with a name (AC/HP optional, kept secret).
- Sheet in a fight: turn order, whose turn, Action / Bonus / Reaction / movement, Dash, Dodge,
  Disengage, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object (each spends the action; Cunning Action as a bonus),
  reactions on anyone's turn (Opportunity Attack, Shield, Uncanny Dodge…), the one-spell-per-turn rule,
  conditions that stay until removed or a long rest, "Hit by a spell?" save helper, End turn.
- Attacks with an unknown AC: Hit / Miss buttons (natural 20 / 1 decide by themselves); damage waits for Hit.
- Typed enemy names as targets (roll_events.target_name).
- Totals show how they were reached ("1d8 (6) + 4 STR = 10"), hit dice too.
- Fight ends: per-turn things reset; HP, conditions and spent slots stay until a rest.
- SQL 34-table-combat.sql.
