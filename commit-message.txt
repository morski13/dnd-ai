Stats: luck index, damage per round per fight, Luck and DPR trend lines

- lib/stats/luck.ts: every d20 scored against its own odds (advantage / disadvantage /
  Elven Accuracy counted), averaged ×100 so 50 = average luck; split by attacks, saves,
  checks and app vs real dice; "could easily be chance" vs "more than chance" verdicts
  (2 standard errors, 10+ rolls); app vs real dice comparison
- lib/stats/dpr.ts: damage that landed in each fight ÷ its rounds, per character and party;
  DPR over a set of fights
- Stats > Whole campaign: Luck index card (bars out from 50, app ● vs real ○ dumbbell,
  tap-for-numbers readout, table view), Damage per round card (party: stacked columns per
  fight; character: their line vs the party average; fight table), Luck and Dmg/round in
  the trend switcher and the side-by-side table
